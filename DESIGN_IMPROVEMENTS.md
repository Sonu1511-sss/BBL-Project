## 🎯 Key Design Principles Applied

1. **Developer-First**
   - Clean, functional design
   - No unnecessary decorations
   - Code-friendly aesthetics

2. **Clear Hierarchy**
   - Important info stands out
   - Proper heading sizes
   - Good use of whitespace

3. **Visual Separation**
   - Free content: Green
   - Paid features: Amber
   - Clear badges and borders

4. **Responsive**
   - Mobile-first approach
   - Breakpoints at sm, md, lg
   - Flexible grids

5. **Accessible**
   - Good contrast ratios
   - Readable fonts
   - Clear labels

## 📐 Design Tokens

### Spacing
- Cards: `p-5` or `p-6`
- Sections: `py-8` or `py-12`
- Gaps: `gap-3` or `gap-4`

### Borders
- Cards: `border border-gray-200`
- Free: `border-l-4 border-l-green-500`
- Paid: `border-l-4 border-l-amber-500`

### Shadows
- Cards: `shadow-card` (subtle)
- Hover: `shadow-card-hover` (slightly more)

### Typography
- Headings: `font-bold` or `font-semibold`
- Body: `font-medium` or regular
- Small: `text-sm` or `text-xs`
